# nit-apps

My personal iPhone apps.

## Daily

My to-do app. Open https://nitpaudel.github.io/nit-apps/daily/ in Safari, then tap Share > Add to Home Screen.

## NitFit

My gym app, built around my own 6-day plan. It tells me the weight and reps for every set, and food tracking comes next. Open https://nitpaudel.github.io/nit-apps/nitfit/ in Safari, then tap Share > Add to Home Screen. Data is backed up to my private nitfit-data repository. Source is in nitfit-src; build with `python3 nitfit-src/build.py nitfit-src nitfit`.

## Settle

Tracks every payment I make from Mom's account and how I pay her back. Open https://nitpaudel.github.io/nit-apps/settle/ in Safari, then tap Share > Add to Home Screen. Backup can go to my private settle-data repository.
